Our Story

Bloom Beauty Parlour opened its doors in Batkhela with a simple aim: to bring city-level beauty standards to clients closer to home, without compromising on hygiene or care.

Since then, we've grown into a studio trusted for bridal preparation, everyday styling and skin treatments, built one appointment at a time on word of mouth from the women of Batkhela.

Certified Staff

Every stylist and makeup artist at Bloom is trained and certified, kept current with modern techniques through ongoing practice.

Client Satisfaction

We take the time to understand what each client wants before a single tool is picked up — the result should feel like you, only more radiant.

Premium Cosmetics

We work only with trusted, skin-safe cosmetic brands, and sanitise every tool between clients as standard practice.

Care you can see

Hygiene isn't an afterthought at Bloom — tools are sanitised between every client, workstations are cleaned throughout the day, and single-use items are never reused. Come as you are; leave glowing.
