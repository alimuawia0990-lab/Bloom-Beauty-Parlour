About Bloom

Our Story

City-level beauty standards, brought closer to home — with thoughtful service, premium products and uncompromising hygiene.

Who We Are

A little more than a salon.

Bloom Beauty Parlour opened its doors in Batkhela with a simple aim: to bring city-level beauty standards to clients closer to home, without compromising on hygiene or care.

Since then, we've grown into a studio trusted for bridal preparation, everyday styling and skin treatments, built one appointment at a time on word of mouth from the women of Batkhela.

Certified Staff

Every stylist and makeup artist at Bloom is trained and certified, with ongoing practice in modern techniques.

Client Satisfaction

We understand what each client wants before a single tool is picked up — the result should feel like you, only more radiant.

Premium Cosmetics

We work with trusted, skin-safe cosmetic brands and sanitise every tool between clients.

Care You Can See

Hygiene is never an afterthought.

Tools are sanitised between every client, workstations are cleaned throughout the day, and single-use items are never reused. Come as you are; leave glowing.
